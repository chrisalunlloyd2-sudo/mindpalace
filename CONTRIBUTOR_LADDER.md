# CONTRIBUTOR_LADDER.md — from visitor to maintainer

> The growth path is explicit so nobody wonders "am I allowed to do
> more?" Every rung has a door that's open right now.

## Rung 0 — Visitor

**You are here.** Things you can do without any commitment:
- Watch the [hosted tour](https://chrisalunlloyd2-sudo.github.io/mind-palace/demo.html)
- Run `--demo` (zero setup)
- Answer the three questions in [Discussion #21](https://github.com/chrisalunlloyd2-sudo/mindpalace/discussions/21)
  — your answers steer the roadmap (the scorecard reads the digest weekly)

## Rung 1 — Tester

**Door: [docs/TEST_PLAN.md](docs/TEST_PLAN.md)** — the 12-surface smoke
pass, 10 minutes. File findings with the bug template. Three accepted
reports put you on [CONTRIBUTORS.md](CONTRIBUTORS.md) with the report
numbers. Your environment row in the matrix is coverage the project
genuinely doesn't have (AMD GPUs, Win11, discrete GPUs).

## Rung 2 — Contributor

**Door: [`good first issue`](https://github.com/chrisalunlloyd2-sudo/mindpalace/issues?q=is%3Aissue+is%3Aopen+label%3A%22good+first+issue%22)**
(#13–#18, all copy-paste runnable) or FIRST_PR.md's fixture-room lane.
After a merged PR: first-PR-author row, and the same access as everyone —
scored backlog tiers, the cascade, the step-log.

Regulars pick their own lane: Tier 1/2 items, layout algorithms,
design asks ([docs/DESIGN.md](docs/DESIGN.md)). The norms are the same
for everyone at every rung ([CONTRIBUTION_NORMS.md](CONTRIBUTION_NORMS.md)).

## Rung 2.5 — Regular

After 3+ merged PRs across areas, you can:
- Triage incoming issues (apply area/priority labels per docs/LABELS.md)
- Propose Tier-2 backlog items with a WHY + spec (maintainer scores them)
- Get write access to branch off and PR without fork overhead

## Rung 3 — Maintainer

**Door: sustained regular work + the Architect's invitation.** A
maintainer: reviews against the cascade, triages the board, scores the
backlog, and keeps the step-log honest. The commitment is time and
evidence discipline, not genius — the norms document applies to
maintainers exactly as to everyone.

## What the program gives back

The autonomous program handles the mechanical work (cascade, evidence
logging, step-log), which is why one person plus contributors can move
a project this size. Your contribution goes through the same
machine-verified gate as everything else — meaning your name on a
shipped change carries the same proof as everything else in the repo.
